# AeroFit Treadmill — Customer Profiling & Probability Analysis

**Domain:** Consumer Goods / Fitness Equipment  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
**Focus:** Descriptive Analytics + Contingency Tables + Conditional Probability

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Aerofit’s market research team wants to understand **who buys which treadmill** (KP281, KP481, KP781) so that store staff can recommend the right product to a new customer based on age, gender, income, fitness level, intended usage, etc.

### Product Portfolio

| Product | Positioning          | Price  |
|---------|----------------------|--------|
| KP281   | Entry-level          | $1,500 |
| KP481   | Mid-level runners    | $1,750 |
| KP781   | Advanced features    | $2,500 |

---

## 🎯 Project Steps

1. Explore and clean the dataset
2. Univariate & bivariate profiling of customers for each product
3. Build two-way **contingency tables**
4. Compute **marginal** and **conditional probabilities**
5. Convert findings into actionable sales recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn

---

## 💡 Example Insights (Typical)

- Higher income + higher fitness customers show elevated probability of purchasing KP781
- Entry-level KP281 is preferred by younger / lower-income segments
- Gender and usage frequency also shift conditional probabilities meaningfully

*(Full probability tables and visualizations are in the notebook.)*
